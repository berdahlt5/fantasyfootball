Add Priority uses fixed positional benchmarks, so it no longer changes merely because a different group of waiver players is available. Role Confidence measures whether the workload is likely to continue; Upside measures projection, latest opportunity, trend, and offensive environment. Players without a positive next-game projection are excluded. A QB listed behind an available starter is also excluded, even when Sleeper supplies a token backup projection; a replacement QB remains eligible when the starter is unavailable. The model uses the last three appearances from a six-week lookback, skips DNPs and byes, reduces Week 18 weight, and discounts old-team usage after a team change. Low-volume touchdowns, isolated long plays, and temporary role spikes remain reliability deductions. QB points use each league's passing-TD, interception, rushing, and fumble settings.
