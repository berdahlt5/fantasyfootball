How the ranking model works
Overall RankRanks teams by offense-only season strength points: actual offensive starter points already scored plus future best-lineup projections. Kicker and D/ST are removed from the model.
Projected FinishCompleted matchups are re-scored using only offensive starters, then each remaining scheduled matchup compares best projected offensive lineups. If a future owned lineup has a zero/empty slot, the optimizer fills that slot with the best eligible unrostered player for that week. This keeps K and D/ST out of the record simulation too.
Starter Position RanksCompleted weeks use each team’s best legal lineup from actual player points. Future weeks use the best legal lineup from projections, with waiver replacement only for slots that would otherwise be zero/empty. Points are credited to the slot used, so FLEX is measured separately.
Roster DepthThere is no hard player-count cap. WR and RB carry the most depth weight because they fill multiple starter/FLEX spots. WR receives a slightly higher baseline dynasty weight because useful receivers generally have longer value windows; RB can still become equally or more valuable when that league’s waiver wire shows stronger RB scarcity. Deeper usable WR/RB assets decay slowly; backup QB/TE assets decay much faster in a normal 1-QB/1-TE league.
Developmental UpsideYoung players can earn a capped prospect bonus from age/experience, NFL draft capital when available, Sleeper depth-chart path, and the projected quality of their NFL offense. Taxi players retain most of that upside even when they are not immediate lineup options.
Waiver Replacement LevelThe model derives each league’s free-agent pool by removing every rostered player from the active player universe. Rostered depth is valued above the best realistically available waiver replacements, so carrying a replaceable QB2 or TE2 adds very little while scarce RB/WR depth retains value.
ScopeProjected finish covers the regular season only. Playoff brackets are not simulated. Whole-roster ranks model QB, RB, WR and TE only; kicker and D/ST are excluded everywhere.
